# Care workflows

[PORTALS.md](PORTALS.md) documents current synthetic registration/linking, check-ins, uploads, sputum diary, daily care, plans, clinician review, sharing and follow-up. Patient images are documentation only. Care-plan instructions are clinician-entered; no automated diagnosis, prescribing or treatment is provided. The clinician Patients directory now links fictional registration, optional account provisioning and the selected care record; the older standalone care route remains for compatibility. A doctor can complete a dated follow-up once with a private note; the original review is retained, an audit event is written, and completed items leave the due queue and summary. This is documentation, not clinical prioritization. The assigned review queue includes open check-ins and uploads, pending sputum/daily entries and due follow-ups, with server pagination, type filtering and timestamp sorting. Its totals are workflow documentation, not clinical urgency; related submissions and follow-ups can appear separately, and pages may shift as records change. Selecting a queue row opens the patient rather than the individual submission; patient detail lists are limited to recent records (100 check-ins/uploads/reviews and 500 sputum/daily entries), so older queued records may not be accessible in those screens. Direct record navigation and other workflow semantics remain open. See [application audit](COMPLETE_APPLICATION_AUDIT.md).
