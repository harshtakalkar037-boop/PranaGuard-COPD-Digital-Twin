# Care workflows

[PORTALS.md](PORTALS.md) documents current synthetic registration/linking, check-ins, uploads, sputum diary, daily care, plans, clinician review, sharing and follow-up. Patient images are documentation only. Care-plan instructions are clinician-entered; no automated diagnosis, prescribing or treatment is provided. The clinician Patients directory now links fictional registration, optional account provisioning and the selected care record; the older standalone care route remains for compatibility. A doctor can complete a dated follow-up once with a private note; the original review is retained, an audit event is written, and completed items leave the due queue and summary. This is documentation, not clinical prioritization. The assigned review queue includes open check-ins and uploads, pending sputum/daily entries and due follow-ups, with server pagination, type filtering and timestamp sorting. Its totals are workflow documentation, not clinical urgency; related submissions and follow-ups can appear separately, and pages may shift as records change. Selecting a queue row opens that exact assigned record, including entries outside the recent history limits; supported review and follow-up actions target the selected ID. Patient history screens now page through check-ins, uploads, shared/clinician reviews, sputum entries and daily activity; clinicians can also page through care-plan versions. Counts and pages share a scoped snapshot, but offset pages may shift as data changes. The older detail endpoints retain their recent-record caps. Other workflow semantics remain open. See [application audit](COMPLETE_APPLICATION_AUDIT.md).
