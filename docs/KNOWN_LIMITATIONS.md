# Known limitations

This is a synthetic research prototype, not a clinically validated medical device. The 72-hour model result is below chance on the documented synthetic evaluation and should not drive diagnosis, triage, prescribing or treatment. Environmental context is city-level, not personal exposure; images are documentation only. The Patients directory now links fictional registration, provisioning and the care record, but general assignment/unlink administration, complete workflow state management and global-route scoping remain unfinished. History pages and queue pages use offsets and may shift as records change. The older patient detail, sputum, daily-care and care-plan APIs retain recent-record caps; the paginated history endpoint must be used to browse older records. Per-username login throttling does not prevent distributed password spraying, registration abuse or targeted account lockout. Manual accessibility and independent security review remain open. Hosting configuration, persistent SQLite/private uploads, backups, provider connectivity, account assignments and live end-to-end behavior are unverified. See [release readiness](FINAL_RELEASE_READINESS.md) and [current audit](COMPLETE_APPLICATION_AUDIT.md).

Care mutations now serialize session and record authorization with database writes, and patient-specific research snapshots and writes recheck access, but other reads, downloads, registry/alert and global operations still have in-flight authorization windows. Patient-link changes away and back or before a stale form submission are not version-detected. Upload staging is not crash-atomic with SQLite; orphan cleanup and operational backup/restore are required. See [authentication and authorization](AUTHENTICATION_AND_AUTHORIZATION.md) for the precise ordering and scope.
