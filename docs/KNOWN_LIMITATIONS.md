# Known limitations

This is a synthetic research prototype, not a clinically validated medical device. The 72-hour model result is below chance on the documented synthetic evaluation and should not drive diagnosis, triage, prescribing or treatment. Environmental context is city-level, not personal exposure; images are documentation only. The Patients directory now links fictional registration, provisioning and the care record, but general assignment/unlink administration, complete workflow state management and global-route scoping remain unfinished. Queue rows can open their exact record, but patient-history lists remain capped and older non-queued records are not generally browsable. Per-username login throttling does not prevent distributed password spraying, registration abuse or targeted account lockout. Manual accessibility and independent security review remain open. Hosting configuration, persistent SQLite/private uploads, backups, provider connectivity, account assignments and live end-to-end behavior are unverified. See [release readiness](FINAL_RELEASE_READINESS.md) and [current audit](COMPLETE_APPLICATION_AUDIT.md).
